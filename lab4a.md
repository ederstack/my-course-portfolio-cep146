## Exercise 3: Academic Integrity Policy (Markdown Version)

# Academic Integrity Policy

## Purpose
The purpose is to promote **honesty**, **trust**, and **fairness** in academic work.

---

## Key Definition

> Academic integrity means acting in an honest, responsible, and ethical manner in all academic activities.

---

## Policy Sections

### 1. General Policy
- Students must complete their own work
- Cheating and plagiarism are not allowed

### 2. Academic Integrity Program
- Schools provide training and resources
- Students must understand citation rules

### 3. Responsibilities
- Students submit original work
- Teachers enforce rules
- School ensures fairness

### 4. Sanctions
- Warning for minor issues
- Zero grade for cheating
- Suspension for repeated violations
